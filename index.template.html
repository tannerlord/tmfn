Planting guide6 steps

Plant it right the first time.

NRCS names grass competition and dry soil as the big hurdles for new trees in North Dakota, not cold. These steps cover a potted tree in a yard or a windbreak row.

  1. Pick the spot

    Check the mature height and spread on the tree's card. Stay clear of power lines, septic fields and foundations, and put windbreaks upwind of what you're protecting.

  2. Dig wide, not deep

    Make the hole two to three times as wide as the pot and no deeper than the root ball, so the tree sits at the same level it grew in the pot.

  3. Loosen the roots

    Slide the tree out and tease apart any roots circling the pot so they grow out into your soil instead of around themselves.

  4. Backfill and soak

    Refill with the soil you dug out, firm it gently by hand, and soak the hole right away to settle out air pockets.

  5. Mulch and kill the grass

    Spread a few inches of wood chips in a wide ring, kept off the trunk, and keep grass and weeds out of that ring until the tree is well established.

  6. Water the first seasons

    Soak deeply once a week whenever it doesn't rain, through the first summer and into fall before freeze-up. Guard against deer and rabbits with a cage or tree shelter.
